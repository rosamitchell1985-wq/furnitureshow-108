Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to a shopping experience that works for everyone.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Our commitment

FurnitureShow is designed with and for older adults and people of all abilities. We believe shopping for furniture should never require young eyes or a technical degree. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA across furnitureshow.site.

What we provide

  • Full keyboard navigation: every feature — menus, filters, checkout — works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a "skip to content" link
  • Screen reader support: semantic headings, labeled form fields, alt text on all images, and ARIA labels on interactive controls
  • High readability: generous line spacing, high-contrast text, and plain-English specifications on every product
  • Plain-language policies: our legal pages are written to be understood, not deciphered

Third-party content

Our payment forms are provided by Stripe, which maintains its own strong accessibility program. While we review every part of the experience, some content supplied by third parties may fall outside our direct control.

Feedback & assistance

Accessibility is a continuous effort, and we welcome your help. If you hit a barrier anywhere on our site, or need help placing an order by phone or email, contact us:

  • 📞 (800) 555-0143 (Mon–Sat, 8am–8pm CT)
  • ✉️ access@furnitureshow.site
  • 📍 FurnitureShow LLC, 1420 Harvest Lane, Suite 200, Austin, TX 78701

We respond to accessibility feedback within two business days and will provide the information or assistance you need through a channel that works for you.
