Cookie Policy

What cookies we use, why, and how to control them.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and remember your preferences. Similar technologies (like local storage) work the same way.

Cookies we use

  • Essential (always on): shopping cart contents, your text-size preference, and security tokens. The Site will not function properly without these.
  • Analytics (optional): help us understand which pages are useful so we can improve the Site. Collected in aggregate, never sold.
  • Marketing (optional): used to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and, with your consent, to show you relevant offers.

Managing cookies

You can change your choices anytime via the cookie banner or your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent checkout from working. Blocking analytics or marketing cookies will not affect your shopping experience.

Third-party services

Stripe processes payments and may set fraud-prevention cookies governed by Stripe's own privacy policy. Our analytics provider processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.

Updates

We may update this policy as our services change; the date above shows the latest revision. Questions: privacy@furnitureshow.site.
